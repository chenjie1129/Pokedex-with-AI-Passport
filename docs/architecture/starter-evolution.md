# Starter evolution

Roster 15: add Ivysaur (2), Charmeleon (5), Wartortle (8). These forms are evolution-only and excluded from normal and Wild encounter pools, including new-place unseen-priority selection.

Choose Bulbasaur, Charmander or Squirtle as buddy, earn at least 30 bond and complete captures together at three distinct saved places. Existing schema 7 bond/place progress counts. Open the starter detail, press OK for actions, and choose EVOLVE to see progress. The screen defaults to LATER; choose EVOLVE and press OK when ready. An inactive buddy is ineligible and the screen explains how to select it. Failed writes offer retry without changing the displayed collection. At success the new form is saved before its 600 ms reveal.

Evolution preserves the starter record and all capture counters. It grants ownership to the target via evolution_obtained, never a synthetic capture or a new capture sequence. The target becomes the buddy and inherits bond/place progress, with target-base stats plus the starter's best stat offsets. Its own previously recorded best stats and progress are preserved if higher. One grant per evolution pair; retries and switching buddies cannot grant it twice.

Schema 8 introduced the evolution-obtained flag in byte 3 of its 20-byte records. Schema 9 expands records for persistent HP; see [persistent HP and release](persistent-hp-and-release.md). Schemas 6/7 with 12 records decode by ID and initialize three new records unknown; v7 buddy fields are preserved. All earlier supported schemas still decode. NVS upgrades the existing key and verifies the write before publishing. Back up before installing; older firmware does not understand the newer payload. Counts shown as OWNED include captures and evolved forms; the total capture counter remains actual catches.

Tests: eligibility boundaries, inactive buddies, failed saves, repeated evolution, original-record preservation, next capture sequence, owned-with-zero-catches codec, actual 276-byte v7 migration, invalid flags, unchanged base encounter pools and Passport evolution goals. Production partial-buffer LVGL renders cover locked/ready/reveal/details for all three forms. The no-save device renderer diagnostic exercises the offer and reveal animation as well as ball rendering. Physical evolution after earning the requirements remains a player acceptance test.
